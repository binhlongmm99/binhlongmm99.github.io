👋 Hi, I'm Long Nguyen! 

I'm currently a PhD Candidate at the School of Electrical Engineering and Robotics, [Queensland University of Technology](https://www.qut.edu.au/) (QUT), supported by a QUT-CSIRO collaborative research scholarship. I am fortunate to be supervised by [Dr. Kien Nguyen Thanh](https://www.qut.edu.au/about/our-people/academic-profiles/k.nguyenthanh) (QUT) and [Prof. Peyman Moghadam](https://people.csiro.au/m/p/peyman-moghadam) (CSIRO Robotics). My research lies at the intersection of 3D vision and robotics, with a focus on developing neural field methods for efficient 3D perception and scene understanding.

Prior to this, I earned my Master’s degree in Computer Science and my Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology at the School of Information and Communication Technology, [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://hust.edu.vn/en/) (HUST), Vietnam, under the supervision of [Prof. Huynh Thi Thanh Binh](https://users.soict.hust.edu.vn/binhht/). During that time, I worked on evolutionary algorithms for combinatorial optimization problems on graphs.
