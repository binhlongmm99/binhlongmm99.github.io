👋 Hi, I'm Long Nguyen! 

I'm currently a PhD Candidate at the School of Electrical Engineering and Robotics, [Queensland University of Technology](https://www.qut.edu.au/) (QUT), supported by a QUT-CSIRO collaborative research scholarship. I am fortunate to be supervised by [Dr. Kien Nguyen Thanh](https://www.qut.edu.au/about/our-people/academic-profiles/k.nguyenthanh) (QUT) and [Prof. Peyman Moghadam](https://people.csiro.au/m/p/peyman-moghadam) (CSIRO's Data61). My research lies at the intersection of 3D vision and robotics, with a focus on developing neural field methods for efficient 3D perception and scene understanding.

Prior to this, I earned my Master’s degree in Computer Science and my Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology at the School of Information and Communication Technology, [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://hust.edu.vn/en/) (HUST), Vietnam, under the supervision of [Prof. Huynh Thi Thanh Binh](https://users.soict.hust.edu.vn/binhht/). I previously worked on evolutionary algorithms for combinatorial optimization problems on graphs, with publications in journals including Applied Soft Computing, Information Sciences, and Knowledge-Based Systems.


🔬 Research directions
======
- *Core expertise*: 3D vision, Neural fields, 3D Gaussian Splatting, Robotics perception
- *Focus areas*: Semantic scene understanding, Vision-language models, Robot-scene interaction
- *Other background*: Evolutionary computation, Combinatorial optimization
